# Gracious Love Academy — Admin Guide

## How to update your school website

This guide will show you how to add news, update photos, change term dates, and edit team members — no coding needed.

---

## Getting started

1. Open your browser and go to:
   `https://graciousloveacademy-school-website.vercel.app/admin/`

2. You will see a login screen. Enter the admin password provided by your web developer.

   **Default password:** `admin2026`

3. After logging in, you will see the **Admin Dashboard** with sections on the left:
   - Homepage
   - Team
   - News
   - Gallery
   - Term Dates

---

## Updating the homepage

1. Click **Homepage** in the left menu.
2. You will see fields for:
   - **Hero Badge** — the small text above the main title
   - **Hero Title** — the big heading on the homepage
   - **Hero Subtitle** — the description under the title
   - **CTA Button Text** — the button label (e.g. "Book a Visit")
   - **Phone Number** — shown on the contact page
   - **Email Address** — shown on the contact page
   - **WhatsApp Link** — for the WhatsApp button
3. Change any text you want.
4. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom.
5. Refresh the homepage to see your changes.

---

## Adding a team member

1. Click **Team** in the left menu.
2. Fill in the form at the top:
   - **Full Name** — e.g. "Mrs. Grace Muthoni"
   - **Role** — e.g. "Headteacher"
   - **Short Bio** — a short description (2–3 sentences)
   - **Years of Experience** — e.g. "12 years experience"
   - **Photo** — click "Choose File" and select a photo from your computer
3. Click **Add Team Member**.
4. The new member will appear immediately in the "Current Team Members" list below.
5. To remove someone, click the **Remove** button next to their name.

**Tip:** Square photos (1:1 ratio) look best for team profiles. JPG or PNG format.

---

## Publishing a news post

1. Click **News** in the left menu.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** — e.g. "PP2 Graduation 2026"
   - **Date** — pick the date from the calendar
   - **Image** — click "Choose File" to upload a photo (optional)
   - **Short Description** — one sentence for the homepage teaser
   - **Full Story** — the complete news article
3. Click **Publish Post**.
4. The post will appear on the News page and the homepage automatically.

**To delete a post:** Find it in the "Published Posts" list and click **Delete**.

---

## Uploading gallery photos

1. Click **Gallery** in the left menu.
2. Click **Choose File** and select a photo from your computer.
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Alt Text** — describe the photo briefly (e.g. "Students doing morning exercises")
   - **Category** — choose from Classroom, Play, Events, or Outdoor
   - **Caption** — optional short label (e.g. "Morning Exercises")
4. Click **Upload to Gallery**.
5. The photo will appear in the gallery on the website immediately.

**Tips:**
- Use photos that are at least 1200px wide for best quality
- Keep file size under 2MB each
- Square or landscape photos work best

---

## Updating term dates and events

1. Click **Term Dates** in the left menu.
2. You will see fields for:
   - **Term 1 Start / End**
   - **Term 2 Start / End**
   - **Term 3 Start / End**
   - **Open Day** — e.g. "Saturday, 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM"
   - **Registration Period** — e.g. "Ongoing"
3. Change any dates or text.
4. Scroll down to **Key Events** to add a new event:
   - Pick a date
   - Enter the event title
   - Add a short description
   - Click **Add Event**
5. Click **Save All Changes** at the bottom.
