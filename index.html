About Gracious Love Academy

A school built on truly knowing each child.

We founded Gracious Love Academy in 2023 with a clear conviction: primary school should feel like a warm, supportive second home. Children learn fastest and develop deep confidence when their teachers know their unique personality, strengths, and pace of growth.

We intentionally maintain a small school environment. Here, your child’s name is spoken with warmth every morning. Their struggles are spotted and corrected early. Their artistic, athletic, and academic milestones are celebrated with genuine pride.

Dedicated teachers who remain with each cohort to foster meaningful mentorship
A balanced daily timetable: focused academics, creative arts, physical play & rest
Continuous, open parent communication — weekly updates, not just end-of-term reports
Safe walled campus, hot nutritious meals, and clean, sanitary facilities

How We Teach the Competency-Based Curriculum

We strictly adhere to the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) framework, delivering it with personalized attention that guarantees mastery before progression.

Stage 1: Exploration & Language

Play Group, PP1 & PP2 focus on spoken language, sensory exploration, phonics sounds, and social development. Children learn to love school through interactive games, storytelling, and motor skill exercises.

Stage 2: Foundational Literacy & Math

Grade 1 to Grade 3 cement solid reading fluency, handwriting, and everyday numeracy without undue pressure. Our small class size ensures teachers listen to each learner read aloud daily.

Stage 3: Depth, Projects & Leadership

Grade 4 to Grade 6 introduce subject-specific discovery, science experiments, digital literacy, and leadership tasks, building full confidence for the national KPSEA assessment and junior secondary.

Core Competencies Built into Every Lesson:

Communication & Collaboration Critical Thinking & Problem Solving Creativity & Imagination Citizenship & National Values Digital Literacy Learning to Learn Self-Efficacy & Moral Character
