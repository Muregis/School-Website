Key Academy Milestones • 2027

NOV 20
2027 Early Admissions & Registration Launch

Enrollment opens for 2027 Play Group, PP1, PP2 and Grades 1–6. Early bird learner assessment.

DEC 5
2027 New Families Open Day & Campus Tour

Guided walkthrough, meet the Headteacher, and uniform fittings for January 2027 intake.

JAN 4
Term 1 2027 Opening Day

Welcome assembly and commencement of lessons for all pupils in the 2027 academic year.

JAN 16
2027 Parents Orientation & CBC Strategy Meeting

Aligning parents and teachers on learner portfolio goals, homework policies, and school van timings.

Scheduled mid-term break for rest and student assessments (resumes 28th June 2027).

Exhibition of pupils' creative arts, science projects, and second term evaluation reports.

Term 3 2027 Opening Day

Final term of the year focusing on curriculum consolidation and KPSEA preparation.

Ministry of Education national summative assessments for candidate learners.

Festive celebration honoring PP2 graduates and recognizing outstanding academic and moral growth.
